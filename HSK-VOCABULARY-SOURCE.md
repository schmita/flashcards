# HSK vocabulary data attribution

The vocabulary database in `hsk-vocabulary.json` is derived from the **Complete HSK Vocabulary** project by Yanis Zafirópulos (Dr.Kameleon).

- Upstream repository: https://github.com/drkameleon/complete-hsk-vocabulary
- Data source: original HSK 2.0 (levels 1–6), exclusive word lists.
- The HSK 7–8 entries are a small, curated advanced extension for this study game; they are not presented as official HSK 2.0 levels.
- Upstream project license: MIT License (copyright notice reproduced below).
